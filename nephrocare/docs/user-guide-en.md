# NephroCare 1.0 – User guide

NephroCare supports people with kidney failure and on dialysis. It works offline and keeps your data only on your phone.

> The app is for education and organising your own information. It does not diagnose, change treatment, or set doses or dry weight. Always follow your nephrologist and dialysis team.

## 1. Install
1. On the phone, open `https://github.com/3ARGOUB3/android/releases/download/nephrocare-latest/nephrocare.apk`
2. Open the downloaded file and, if asked, allow installs from this source (Samsung: Settings › Apps › Special access › Install unknown apps).
3. Tap Install. Android 6.0 or newer.

## 2. First start
Choose the language, read and accept the medical safety notice, enter your name (optional) and allow notifications.

## 3. Sections
- **Home**: current weight vs dry weight, next session, last blood pressure, today's medicines with a Taken button, fluids today, tip of the day, tiles to every section.
- **Dry weight**: pre/post/home weights and the prescribed dry weight; gain between sessions in kg and % of dry weight; charts and history.
- **Blood pressure**: systolic, diastolic, pulse and context; charts, pre/post averages, alerts for very high or very low readings.
- **Labs**: 11 tests with the lab's unit (automatic conversion), simple explanation, indicative ranges, chart and history.
- **Nutrition**: guide, 78-food table, 17 Algerian dishes, recipes and a sample day.
- **Fistula**: illustration, daily thrill and infection check, care articles.
- **Dialysis schedule**: days, time, reminders, bag reminder the evening before, session log, bag checklist.
- **Medicines**: name, dose copied from the prescription, times, reminders with a Taken action, history, optional stock tracking (lowered by each dose, days left, reminder to renew the prescription, Refill button); the app never suggests doses.
- **Prayer & adhkar**: offline prayer times with reminders, adhkar with counters, duas for the sick, full Quran with translations.
- **Encyclopedia, support, emergency**: 70 articles per language; emergency call buttons.
- **Search**, **media** (illustrations, your videos with subtitles), **diary** (symptoms, questions for the doctor), **fluids** (allowance set by your team; optional reminders every 2–4 h with +100/+200 ml buttons), **centers** (your own directory with call and map; CSV import/export so an association can share one list – template in `docs/centres-template.csv`), **PDF reports** to share with the doctor.

## 4. Settings and privacy
Language, light/dark theme, text size, notification and exact-alarm permissions (on Samsung set the app's battery use to Unrestricted), medicine names in notifications (off by default), PIN lock, password-encrypted backup and restore, delete all data.
